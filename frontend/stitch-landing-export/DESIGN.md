## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an unapologetic, high-octane neobrutalist aesthetic tailored strictly for institutional-grade financial analysis and intelligence. It rejects the sterile, homogenized corporate SaaS palette in favor of tactile immediacy, ultra-clear visual demarcation, and expressive geometric authority.

The brand persona is audacious, radically honest, hyper-performant, and structurally rigorous. Designed for traders, financial engineers, and strategic analysts who spend hours staring at complex data streams, the system bridges extreme graphic contrast with razor-sharp utilitarian clarity. The tactile physics of offset hard shadows, physical stamp-like containers, and heavy structural scaffolding provide instant spatial mapping, preventing eye fatigue across dense layouts.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-tension chromatic contract: stark monochrome foundations offset by saturated, punchy industrial neons. 

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** The root canvas is anchored to an archival cream (`#FFFDF5`), softening glare while maintaining stark contrast against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card panels. Structural ink is absolute pitch black (`#000000`), used unsparingly for 2px–3px borders, hard shadows, and typography.
- **Accents & Roles:**
  - **Electric Gold (`#FFE600`):** Primary command surfaces, primary CTAs, active tools, and primary system callouts.
  - **Terminal Blue (`#0055FF`):** Secondary interactive triggers, links, highlighted table columns, and structural tabs.
  - **Signal Magenta / Neo-Pink (`#FF3366`):** Critical operational states, downward delta/loss indicators, and margin liquidation warnings.
  - **Hyper Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** Informational banners, secondary metrics, and liquidity trackers.
  - **Matrix Green (`#00E599`):** Upward delta/gain, validated trades, healthy collateral statuses, and success notifications.
  - **Industrial Hazard Orange (`#FF7A00`):** Cautionary alerts, unconfirmed ledger transfers, and pending execution orders.

Background surfaces across data grids should remain clean white or cream; saturated fills are reserved strictly for badges, key action anchors, summary metric tags, and active states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the raw geometric rhythm of **Space Grotesk** with the surgical precision of **JetBrains Mono**.

- **Editorial & Structural Hierarchy:** Space Grotesk is deployed with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on titles and headers to generate a solid, blocky poster aesthetic. Section titles and card headers should lean toward uppercase or bold sentence-case to establish absolute hierarchy over data fields.
- **Financial & Quantitative Data:** JetBrains Mono is strictly mandatory for all asset balances, tickers, timestamps, cryptographic hashes, transaction volumes, and table cell values. Tabular numerals (`tnum`) ensure numbers do not jitter during real-time streaming updates.
- **Metadata & Labels:** `label-caps` enforces high-legibility uppercase notation for table headers, status pills, and input labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an uncompromising, rigid 12-column modular grid that favors high density and visual order over ethereal white space.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) external frame padding. Multi-pane analytical dashboards tile seamlessly with uniform border joints.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 24px margins. Sub-panels fold into tabbed containers.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px gutters, and 16px margins. Financial widgets stack vertically into monolithic cards.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-spacing relies on an 8px base rhythm with 4px half-steps. Spacing between interrelated data blocks should stay tight (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) to maximize visible information density, relying on 2px black dividing rules rather than excessive blank space to differentiate metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system completely forbids blurred, ambient, or soft drop shadows, as well as semi-transparent frosted glass layers. Depth is strictly tactile, mechanical, and binary.

- **Hard Angular Shadows:** All elevated elements project solid, pure black (`#000000`) hard drop shadows with zero blur radius, oriented down-and-right at 45 degrees:
  - **Static Base (Cards, Panels, Modals):** `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000;`
  - **Interactive Standout (Buttons, Badges, Dropdowns):** `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #000000;`
  - **Deep Overlays (System Modals, Command Palette):** `box-shadow: 8px 8px 0px #000000;`
- **Border Scaffolding:** Every container, card, button, and input carries an uninterrupted 2px or 3px solid black border (`border: 2px solid #000000;`).
- **Tactile Kinematics (Hover & Active States):** 
  - On hover, interactive controls shift up and left by 1px with an expanded shadow (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000; transform: translate(-1px, -1px);`).
  - On active/pressed states, the element is depressed down and right directly into its footprint (`box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #000000; transform: translate(3px, 3px);`), giving the visceral response of a mechanical switch.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strict, non-negotiable **Sharp (`0px`)** geometry across all components.

- **Zero Radius:** Modals, cards, buttons, dropdowns, tooltips, tags, and inputs must use `border-radius: 0px`. Curves conflict with the brutalist architectural metaphor.
- **Notched & Chamfered Variations:** As an optional decorative cue for primary terminal badges or cryptographic key containers, 45-degree corner chamfers (cut corners using CSS `clip-path`) of 6px to 8px may be applied to reinforce high-tech utility.
- **Segmented Separators:** Internal dividers within cards and tables use continuous 2px horizontal and vertical rules that lock flush against parent container borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Electric Gold (`#FFE600`), 2px solid black border, 3px 3px hard black shadow, Space Grotesk bold text. Hover shifts -1px/-1px with 4px shadow; pressed state collapses shadow to 0px with a 3px translate offset.
- **Secondary:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or Neo-Cyan (`#38BDF8`) fill with identical 2px black border and 3px 3px shadow.
- **Destructive:** Signal Magenta (`#FF3366`) fill with white or black typography, reserved for liquidations, emergency cancellations, and irreversible orders.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 2px solid black border and 4px 4px 0px black shadow.
- Card headers feature an explicit bottom border (`border-bottom: 2px solid #000000`) separating the title and control tools from the data viewport. Card titles are styled with `title-sm` or `title-md` and accompanied by a colored square icon or status badge.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- Sharp rectangular fields with an off-white or pure white fill, framed in 2px black.
- Focus state switches the border to 3px solid black with an immediate yellow glow or accent tint (`background-color: #FFFDF5; box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #000000`).
- Text inside inputs uses `data-mono-md` for numerical values and `body-md` for text queries.

### Financial Data Tables
- Strict grid styling: every cell is bound by a 1px solid black border, with column header rows styled in 2px black borders and an Electric Gold or cream background.
- Row hover triggers an immediate flat color tint (e.g., `#FFE600` at 15% opacity or pure `#F4F0EA`), never an elevation shift.
- Numbers are right-aligned, monospaced (`JetBrains Mono`), and color-coded only for delta changes (`#00E599` for positive, `#FF3366` for negative).

### Badges, Tags & Status Pills
- Compact sharp tags (`border-radius: 0px`) with 1.5px solid black borders and 2px 2px 0px hard shadows.
- Distinct color coding: "BULLISH/ACTIVE" in Matrix Green (`#00E599`), "BEARISH/RISK" in Neo-Pink (`#FF3366`), "PENDING" in Hazard Orange (`#FF7A00`), and "SYSTEM/TAG" in Terminal Blue (`#0055FF`) with contrasting black text.
- Text rendered in `label-caps` for instant scanning.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 18x18px square with a 2px solid black border, sharp corners, and white background. When checked, the fill snaps to Electric Gold (`#FFE600`) with a bold black checkmark.
- Radios: 18x18px square (preserving brutalist angularity instead of circles), filled with a centered black square pip when active.
